What happens after submit

  1. 1n8n receives the webhook
  2. 2Customer data is validated and normalized
  3. 3NVIDIA NIM extracts structured service context
  4. 4The local development CRM boundary saves the request
  5. 5A follow-up is scheduled for email-capable leads
  6. 6Booking saves the appointment, cancels the follow-up, and sends a local confirmation
Website→n8n→AI enrichment→Saved request→Mailpit

AI enrichment has a safe fallback; lead intake does not depend on AI availability.
